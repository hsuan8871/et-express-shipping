EZ WAY 易利委 — 台灣海關必要程序Taiwan Customs Required
⚠️ 2026 年 3 月起全面強制實施⚠️ Mandatory since March 2026
所有進口台灣快遞包裹，收件人必須完成 EZ WAY 實名認證並及時確認申報，否則包裹將被扣留、無法通關，並從第 4 天起產生倉租費。 All courier packages entering Taiwan require EZ WAY real-name registration. Failure to confirm in the app will result in customs hold and storage fees from day 4.

完成實名認證Complete registration 只需一次One-time
填寫姓名、身分證字號、手機號碼，選擇認證方式：

電信認證（推薦）：本人門號申辦（中華/遠傳/台哥大/亞太/台灣之星），關閉 WiFi 以 4G/5G 行動網路驗證，約 30 分鐘內完成。

簡訊認證：非本人門號或預付卡，需上傳身分證照片，約 1–3 個工作天。

⚠️ 請使用台灣境內網路操作。部分 VPN 可能被系統偵測而無法完成認證。 Enter your name, National ID, and mobile number. Choose a method:

Telecom verification (recommended): For numbers registered in your name (major carriers). Turn off WiFi, use 4G/5G. Completes within ~30 minutes.

SMS verification: For prepaid or non-personal numbers. Upload ID photo, wait 1–3 business days.

⚠️ Use a Taiwan IP address. Some VPNs may be detected and block registration.

台灣進口規定 & 關稅Taiwan Import Rules & Duties
免稅額：完稅價格 NT$2,000（約 €55）以下免稅Duty-free: taxable value under NT$2,000 (~€55)
完稅價格 = 商品價值 + 運費 + 保險。超過則課關稅（依品項 0–12%）+ 營業稅 5%。
每人每半年最多 6 次免稅進口，第 7 次起不論金額都要課稅。所有稅款由收件人負擔。 Taxable value = goods + shipping + insurance. Duties (0–12%) + 5% VAT apply above threshold.
Maximum 6 duty-free imports per half-year per person. From the 7th, duties apply regardless of value. All taxes are the recipient's responsibility.

1. 申報責任Declaration Responsibility

客戶須如實申報包裹內容物及申報價值（上限 €1,200）。因申報不實導致的海關查驗、扣押、罰款，由客戶自行承擔。 The sender must accurately declare contents and value (max €1,200). Customs issues from inaccurate declarations are the sender's responsibility.

2. 禁寄物品Prohibited Items

  • 酒精類（含香水）、肉類製品、3C 電子產品Alcohol (incl. perfume), meat products, electronics/3C
  • 藥品、含管制成分保健品（CBD、褪黑激素等）Medicines, supplements with controlled substances
  • 仿冒品、獨立鋰電池、現金、危險品Counterfeit goods, standalone batteries, cash, dangerous goods

3. 賠償（包丟不包損）Compensation (Loss only)

未投保：依 DHL 標準條款約 22 SDR/kg（約 €26/kg）。已投保：賠付至申報價值上限 €1,200。所有賠償以 DHL 實際核准金額為準，ET Express 不額外補償。包裝不當或易碎品損壞不在賠償範圍。 Without insurance: ~22 SDR/kg (~€26/kg). With insurance: up to declared value, max €1,200. All compensation subject to DHL's actual assessment. ET Express provides no additional compensation. Damage from improper packaging is excluded.

4. 退款政策Refund Policy

未出單：退款扣 €5 手續費。已出單未投遞：退款扣 €5（需 DHL 取消確認）。已投遞：不退款。退款申請須於付款後 48 小時內書面提出，逾期不受理。 Label not generated: refund minus €5. Label generated, not delivered: refund minus €5 (pending DHL cancellation). Delivered: no refund. Requests must be within 48 hours of payment.

5. 時效Transit Time

通常 2–4 個工作天。海關查驗、天災、節假日等因素導致的延誤不在 ET Express 責任範圍。 Typically 2–4 business days. Delays from customs, natural disasters, or holidays are outside ET Express's liability.

6. 付款Payment

僅接受歐元 IBAN 銀行轉帳，先付後寄。 EUR bank transfer (IBAN) only. Payment required before label generation.

7. EZ WAY 責任EZ WAY Responsibility

收件人須自行完成 EZ WAY 實名認證並及時確認申報。因未完成 EZ WAY 程序導致的延誤、倉租費、退件，由客戶自行承擔。 The recipient is responsible for completing EZ WAY registration and confirming declarations. ET Express is not liable for delays, storage fees, or returns from incomplete EZ WAY procedures.

8. 進口稅Import Duties
